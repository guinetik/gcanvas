Rubik's Cube

27 cubelets that scramble and solve themselves.

A 3x3x3 cube with animated layer rotations, slowly turning while it waits for orders.

Each cubelet is a Cube3D projected through a Camera3D, with Button driving the moves.

  • Shuffle scrambles with random layer moves
  • Solve replays the moves in reverse
  • Drag rotates the camera
  • Double-click resets the view
