Thomas Attractor

René Thomas' cyclically symmetric attractor, 1999.

Three sines, one damping term, elegant three-fold symmetry. Trails are colored by speed: blue = slow, green = fast.

dx/dt = sin(y) − bx
dy/dt = sin(z) − by
dz/dt = sin(x) − bz
b = 0.208186
