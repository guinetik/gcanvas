Fluid Dynamics · Ink Flow

Push the fluid. Watch the ink curl and mix.

The colors are ink carried by a moving fluid. Drag to push it across the screen, or hold in one spot to spin up a little whirlpool. The tiny moving streaks help you follow the current.

Automatic stirring keeps fresh ink flowing. Turn Auto stir down to zero to paint freely. Palettes recolor the ink already on screen; higher ink quality preserves finer ribbons.

The Navier–Stokes equations describe the flow: pressure keeps it from bunching up, viscosity smooths it, and your gestures add a push.

  • Drag / swipe push fluid and add ink
  • Tap / hold add a swirl; a new stroke changes ink color
  • Shift reverse the swirl
  • Space pause or resume; touching the fluid also resumes
  • R start with fresh ink
  • H show or hide controls, at the top right
