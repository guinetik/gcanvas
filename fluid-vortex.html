Fluid Vortex — A live fluid, written in light.

Drag to stir. Hold to spin up a local eddy. Space pauses, R resets, H hides the interface. On mobile, tap Controls for settings.

This experiment connects GCanvas's 2D SPH fluid forces to the attractor trail renderer. Pressure and viscosity couple neighboring particles; an external vortex drive keeps the liquid circulating. Every line records a particle's actual recent motion.

Try Twin currents for interacting eddies or Free ink to remove the vortex drive. This is a stylized particle simulation with a circular boundary, separate from the analytic Navier–Stokes vortex. Bloom and glow are artistic effects.
