Rabinovich–Fabrikant Attractor

Modulation instability in a dissipative medium, 1979.

A stiff system of nested petal-like loops, integrated with RK4. Cyan marks the gliding arcs, hot pink the sharp turns.

dx/dt = y(z − 1 + x²) + γx
dy/dt = x(3z + 1 − x²) + γy
dz/dt = −2z(α + xy)
α = 0.14   γ = 0.10
