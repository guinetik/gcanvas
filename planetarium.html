Planetarium

A solar system on real Keplerian rails.

The sun, planets, and moons orbit with proper eccentricity, plus an optional spacetime curvature grid.

Shaded Sphere3D bodies through a Camera3D, with Gesture handling zoom.

  • Drag rotates the view
  • Scroll/pinch zooms
  • Space pauses the simulation
  • Double-click resets the view
