Gaussian Wave Packet

A free quantum particle, drawn as a helix.

Re(Ψ) and Im(Ψ) spiral around the axis of motion; the red envelope traces |Ψ|² and the blue projection shows the real part alone. Hold still and the packet collapses — a stylized measurement.
