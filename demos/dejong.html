De Jong Attractor

Peter de Jong's swirling cousin of the Clifford map.

A quarter-million points iterate the map on the GPU while a slowly wobbles, morphing the swirl; speed sets the hue — cyan = slow, orange = fast.

yₖ₊₁ = sin(c·xₖ) − cos(d·yₖ)
