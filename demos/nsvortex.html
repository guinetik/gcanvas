Navier–Stokes Vortex

A shrinking vortex, wrapped in glowing waves.

The bright core tightens while ribbons around it stretch, twist and fade. This is a generative artwork inspired by OpenAI’s Navier–Stokes paper.

The faint outline remembers the vortex’s starting shape and turns with your drag. Follow core magnifies the live core while leaving that outline at its starting scale. Switch to Collapse · fixed scale to compare their actual sizes. On larger screens, the small Fixed scale view also compares the starting and current sizes. The timeline lets you explore the same moment from different angles.

Core radius ∝ τ0.5
Core speed ∝ τ−0.505

τ means time remaining before the paper’s limiting event. These scales guide the artwork; the ribbons are drawn, not computed by a fluid solver.

  • Drag orbit the vortex
  • Scroll zoom
  • Space pause or resume
  • R restart the timeline
  • H hide or show the interface
  • Controls open settings on a small screen
