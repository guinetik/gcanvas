Bezier Curve Editor

Click out a curve, then bend it to your will.

Control points become path commands — line, quadratic, cubic — and the curve rebuilds live as you edit. Cut shapes stay on canvas, wobbling.

BezierShape defines the path with M/L/Q/C commands, and ShapeGOFactory wraps shapes into interactive GameObjects.

  • Click place a control point (Add mode)
  • Drag move a point (Edit mode)
  • Cut detach the curve and let it jitter
  • Clear wipe the canvas
