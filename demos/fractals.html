Fractals

A fractal explorer rendered off the main thread.

Mandelbrot, Julia, Newton and friends are computed in a web worker and streamed back as bitmaps; pan and zoom glide with eased momentum.

Fractals supplies the math, TaskManager runs it in a worker, and Painter.img with ImageGo puts the pixels on screen.

  • Drag pan with momentum
  • Scroll zoom toward the cursor
  • Pinch zoom on touch
  • Buttons cycle type, iterations, color — animate, zoom
