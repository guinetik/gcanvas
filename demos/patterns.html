Pattern Gallery

Procedural pixel textures, tiled to taste.

A scrollable grid of seamless patterns — dots, voronoi, penrose, perlin noise — each generated pixel by pixel at load with random colors.

Patterns generates the raw pixels, Painter.img turns them into bitmaps, and PatternRectangle tiles them inside a scrollable TileLayout.
