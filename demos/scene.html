Scene Layers

Fifty draggable squares fighting for the front.

Colored squares scatter across a layered Scene; grab one and it pops to the top of the draw order.

Scene layering with bringToFront, the applyDraggable mixin, Random distributions, and Button UI in a VerticalLayout.

  • Drag move a square and bring it to front
  • Hover grow a square
  • Buttons add, clear, and re-scatter layers
