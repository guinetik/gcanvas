Chua's Circuit

The first physical circuit proven chaotic, 1983.

Leon Chua's piecewise-linear diode folds two spiralling lobes across a saddle — the double scroll. Cyan marks the slow spiral arms, green the fast lobe transitions.

dx/dt = α(y − x − G(x))
dy/dt = x − y + z
dz/dt = −γy
G(x) = m₁x + ½(m₀ − m₁)(|x+1| − |x−1|)
α = 15.6   γ = 25.58   m₀ = −2   m₁ = 0
