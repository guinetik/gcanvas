Aizawa Attractor

A chaotic torus that keeps folding through itself.

Orbits wrap the torus and spike out along its axis, colored by speed: cyan = slow, magenta = fast.

dx/dt = (z − b)x − dy
dy/dt = dx + (z − b)y
dz/dt = c + az − z³/3 − (x² + y²)(1 + ez) + fzx³
a = 0.95   b = 0.7   c = 0.6   d = 3.5   e = 0.25   f = 0.1
