Particles

Five hundred primitives that fear your cursor.

A swarm of random circles, squares, triangles, and stars bouncing with friction, spin, and trails. Each particle is its own GameObject running its own physics.

GameObject particles rendering cached Shape primitives in a Scene, with a Button panel in a VerticalLayout.

  • Move repels nearby particles
  • Drag spawns new particles
  • Clear / Reset buttons empty or respawn the swarm
