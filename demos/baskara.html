Bhaskara Dance

The quadratic formula, set loose on the plane.

Each particle pair traces the roots of a quadratic as its coefficients animate. Roots merge when the discriminant hits zero and spiral into the complex plane when it goes negative.

ax² + bx + c = 0  →  x = (−b ± √Δ) / 2a,   Δ = b² − 4ac
