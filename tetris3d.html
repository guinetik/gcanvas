3D Tetris

Tetris, but the well has three dimensions.

Pieces fall into a 6×6×16 well — fill a whole layer to clear it. The View button cycles preset camera angles.

  • WASD / Arrows move piece
  • Q/E R/F Z/C rotate on each axis
  • Space hard drop
  • Enter start · Esc pause
  • Drag rotate camera
