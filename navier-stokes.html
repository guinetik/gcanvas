Navier–Stokes Playground — A study of vortex motion.

Drag freely to orbit in any direction. Scroll to zoom. Choose Slow or Fast in Motion, or adjust Auto Move (0 turns it off). Space pauses motion, R resets, H hides the interface. On mobile, tap Controls to open the panel.

This Burgers vortex is a smooth, steady solution of the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations. Fluid spirals inward and stretches along its axis while viscosity spreads the rotating core. Color follows angular rotation; the Copper Tide palette uses blue for the outer flow and gold for the fast core. Values use normalized units.

Open Color & light for six palettes and the Filament, Neon, and Nebula looks. View has separate bloom and glow controls. Glow and iridescent shimmer are artistic effects.

Inspired by the September 2026 announcement. This art study illustrates vortex motion, not a singularity proof.
