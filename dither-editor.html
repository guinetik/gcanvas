Dither Editor

Turn any photo into inky halftone pixels.

Load an image, shape its tones, and dither it with any of eleven algorithms in real time. Free Pixels scatters the result into 3D particles.

Dither supplies the algorithms, image-adjust helpers shape the tones, and an AccordionGroup of Slider and Dropdown controls drives it all.

  • Drop load an image, or use the Load Image button
  • Scroll zoom
  • Drag pan when zoomed in
  • Compare toggle original vs processed
  • Free Pixels explode the image into draggable 3D particles
  • Export PNG save the result
